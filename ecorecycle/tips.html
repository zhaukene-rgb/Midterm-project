Sorting made simple

What goes in each bin, and which plastics you can use again.

Plastic

Rinse, remove caps and flatten. Skip greasy takeaway boxes.

Paper

Keep it dry and clean. Wet or waxed paper does not recycle.

Glass

Bottles and jars only. Mirrors and window glass are different.

Batteries

Tape the ends and keep them in a jar until you reach a point.

Which plastics can I reuse?

Look for the number inside the triangle on the bottom of the item.

Code Type Examples Reuse?
1 PET Water and soda bottles No, recycle after one use
2 HDPE Milk jugs, shampoo bottles Yes, many times
3 PVC Pipes, cling film No, avoid
4 LDPE Shopping bags Yes, until they tear
5 PP Food tubs, lunch boxes Yes, heat-resistant
6 PS Foam cups and trays No, avoid
7 Other Mixed plastics Check the label first

Quick answers

Do I need to wash everything?

A quick rinse is enough. Food residue is the main reason recyclables get rejected.

What about plastic bags?

Reuse them first. Clean bags can go to store collection bins.

Where do old gadgets go?

Electronics points take phones, chargers and small appliances. For big items, request a pickup.
