Small details.
Better sorting.

A few things to know before you go.

Empty containers and remove food residue. Follow your collection point’s instructions for rinsing and preparation.

Check the point’s rules. Some accept caps attached; others collect them separately. There is no single rule for every point.

Look for dedicated battery or electronics collection. Start with the map on the drop-off points page and confirm what the location accepts.

Arrange collection with a real local provider. You can explore the process using our pickup request demo; it does not book a courier.
