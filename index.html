Sort your waste. Find where it goes.

Plastic, paper, glass, batteries and old electronics can all be recycled. EcoRecycle shows what goes where and the nearest place to drop it off.

Find a drop-off point

Where does it go?

  • Plastic bottle: plastic point
  • Newspaper: paper point
  • Glass jar: glass point
  • Old battery: battery point, never the bin
  • Old phone: electronics point

Why it matters

Less landfill

Most household waste can be reused as new material if it is sorted at home.

Cleaner water

One thrown-away battery can pollute a lot of water with toxic metals.

Saved resources

Recycling paper saves trees, and recycling glass saves sand and energy.

Three simple steps

1. Sort at home

Keep separate containers for plastic, paper, glass and batteries.

2. Pick a point

Check the table to see what each point accepts and when it is open.

3. Too big to carry?

For old furniture or appliances, request a pickup.
